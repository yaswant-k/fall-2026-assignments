# Fall 2026 Assignments — UMBC (private study workspace)

Self-contained prompt packs, drafts, and prep sheets for IS 369, IS 472, and IS 430.
Nothing in this repo is submitted anywhere — every file is a draft or a prompt for the
student's own review and execution. Nothing is submitted to Blackboard without a
per-item go-ahead from the student.

## Layout
- `is369/` — prompt pack (all 36 gradebook items: rubrics, instructions, question-topic
  summaries, deliverable formats, paste-ready prompts). The three items the IS 369
  instructor ruled out (IT-1 Student Quiz 1, Presentation 1, Article Review 1) are
  excluded. IT-3 has no posted question document or article as of Oct 30, 2026.
- `is472/` — prompt pack, Exercise 4–7 code drafts, Exercise 2 abuse-case draft, quiz prep.
- `is430/` — prompt pack, HW1–HW3 lab walkthroughs and report skeletons, exam prep.
- `assignment-tracker.md` — every item, its status, and what is still needed.

## Honest scope notes (read before using)
- The packs target every grading criterion listed for each item. They do not restore
  points already recorded, and they do not override instructor judgment — IS 472 has no
  pre-defined grade boundaries, and IS 430/IS 369 late and makeup terms are the
  instructors' decisions.
- IS 369 ceiling on the inferred makeup mapping: max 754.55 / 1000 (75.5%, a C), needing
  92.1% across makeup + future work. A 100% overall course grade is not achievable from
  the recorded state, and no file here claims otherwise.
- IS 430 lab handouts prohibit generative AI on the labs and require screenshots from
  the student's own SEED VM runs. The IS 430 prompts are therefore guided-execution
  prompts: they walk through the lab and check real outputs; they must not invent
  screenshots, outputs, or observations. That rule is part of the assignment
  requirements and is kept in the packs deliberately.
- Question lists and course materials in these files are summarized in the author's own
  words, not reproduced verbatim. Journal articles, handouts, and slide decks are not
  included; only citations and summaries. Add your own copies of the articles and
  documents to the `[FILL]` placeholders before running a prompt.
